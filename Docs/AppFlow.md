# App Flow — Portfolio Experience

## 1. Goal of the flow

The site should feel like a guided conversation with a clear structure. A visitor should quickly understand:
- who the developer is,
- what kind of work they do,
- what they care about,
- how to contact them,
- and why the chatbot exists.

## 2. First impression

When the page loads, the visitor should immediately see:
- the name or brand,
- a direct role statement,
- a concise summary of the work,
- a clear next action.

The first screen should answer the question: “What does this person do?”

## 3. Navigation path

Primary navigation should help visitors jump to:
- Now,
- Work or Projects,
- Thinking,
- Contact.

The page should still make sense if someone scrolls top to bottom without using the navbar.

## 4. Suggested reading order

### Step 1: Hero
The hero establishes identity and tone. It should be memorable without being loud.

### Step 2: About
The about section gives a fuller explanation of background and current work.

### Step 3: Now
The Now section shows what the developer is focused on right now. This makes the site feel maintained and current.

### Step 4: Experience
This section proves there is real work behind the portfolio. It should show results and working style.

### Step 5: Education
This is short and grounded. It confirms academic context without making it the main story.

### Step 6: Process
This section explains how the developer thinks and works. It gives confidence to clients and recruiters.

### Step 7: Projects
This is where the strongest proof lives. Projects should be easy to skim but deep enough to reward attention.

### Step 8: Stack decisions
This section communicates judgment. It is especially useful for technically minded visitors.

### Step 9: Skills
This section summarizes technical comfort zones without turning into a keyword dump.

### Step 10: Micro opinions
This is the personality layer. It makes the site feel like a real human wrote it.

### Step 11: Contact
The visitor ends with a direct path to reach out.

## 5. Chatbot flow

The chatbot should be discoverable in two ways:
- a floating launcher,
- a CTA in the hero.

The chatbot should support questions like:
- What is Hisaab Pro?
- Why did you use this stack?
- How do you find freelance clients?
- What are you working on now?

## 6. Chat experience flow

### Entry
The user clicks the chat button or CTA.

### Prompting
The UI shows a few quick prompts so the user knows what to ask.

### Conversation
The user sends a question and gets a streamed answer.

### Response behavior
The response should:
- stay grounded in portfolio content,
- be concise,
- feel personal,
- avoid generic AI phrasing.

## 7. Interaction principles

- Do not force the visitor to read everything.
- Do not hide important information behind extra clicks.
- Do not make the chatbot necessary to understand the portfolio.
- The chatbot should deepen the experience, not replace the page.

## 8. Visitor journeys

### Recruiter journey
1. Scans hero.
2. Reads projects and experience.
3. Checks stack decisions.
4. Skims contact.
5. May ask the chatbot about architecture or work style.

### Client journey
1. Scans hero.
2. Reads about and process sections.
3. Checks freelance-related experience.
4. Looks at projects.
5. Uses chatbot to ask about fit or past work.

### Technical peer journey
1. Skims hero.
2. Opens projects and stack decisions.
3. Reads micro opinions.
4. Uses chatbot to ask deeper questions about decisions.

## 9. Conversion points

The site should create contact opportunities at several points:
- hero CTA,
- contact section,
- floating chat,
- footer.

## 10. Content behavior across flow

The content should build confidence in layers:
- identity,
- proof,
- judgment,
- personality,
- availability.

## 11. Mobile flow

On mobile, the content must still read cleanly in order:
- hero first,
- then about/now,
- then work,
- then contact.

Navigation should be collapsed but still usable.

## 12. Success criteria

The flow works if:
- a visitor understands the person quickly,
- the site feels coherent from top to bottom,
- the chatbot is easy to discover,
- the contact path is obvious,
- the page feels alive rather than static.
